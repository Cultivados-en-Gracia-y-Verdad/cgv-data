# Prophecy Chronology

This directory stores chronological relations explicitly stated in Scripture and preserves uncertain placement without forcing a completed prophetic timeline.

## Purpose

The chronology layer answers questions such as:

- What does the passage place first, next, afterward, during, or at the end?
- Which event is tied to a named reign, year, duration, midpoint, or boundary?
- Which events are explicitly stated to occur together?
- Which cross-passage placements remain candidates rather than established identities?

## Chronology basis types

- `historical_anchor` — a named reign, year, ruler, location, or narrative setting.
- `relative_order` — before, after, then, until, afterward, at that time, or equivalent original-language sequencing.
- `duration` — a stated period, count, midpoint, beginning, or ending boundary.
- `passage_sequence` — the order of events within a dream, vision, discourse, or narrative.
- `explicit_fulfillment` — Scripture itself states that an earlier word or event is fulfilled.
- `candidate_alignment` — two separately established sequences may correspond, but Scripture has not yet been shown to equate them.

## Placement status

- `explicit` — the relation is stated by the cited passage.
- `derived-within-passage` — assembled only from the passage's own ordered statements.
- `provisional` — useful working placement that requires further textual evidence.
- `unplaced` — the event is recorded, but its location on the broader spine is not established.
- `disputed-internally` — competing textual placements remain under review; no decision is stored as settled.

## Governing rules

1. Establish each passage's internal order before connecting it to another passage.
2. Do not convert a shared word, number, title, or image into chronological identity.
3. Preserve the difference between the time of revelation and the time described by the revelation.
4. Preserve named durations in their stated units until Scripture establishes a conversion.
5. Preserve pronouns and unidentified persons as unresolved.
6. A whole-canon spine is an assembly view, not authority over the source records.
7. Every displayed placement must expose its source reference, basis type, and status.
8. Events may remain in parallel lanes until Scripture supplies a connection.

`../studies/whole-canon-chronological-spine.yaml` is the continually expanding assembly view. It is intended to keep the research visible and engaging while the detailed files are gathered over time.
