# Unresolved Questions and Assumptions

This directory preserves claims, inherited models, labels, and possible alignments that must not be treated as settled research.

## Purpose

An assumption file allows the research to ask a real question without importing its answer into passage, event, connection, or chronology records.

## Status values

- `unexamined` — recorded but not yet systematically quarried.
- `collecting` — relevant passages and original-language data are being gathered.
- `partially-supported` — some elements are explicit, but the complete claim is not established.
- `limited` — Scripture establishes less than the claim asserts.
- `contradicted` — a cited textual statement conflicts with the claim.
- `resolved-supported` — the complete claim has been established by the recorded evidence.
- `resolved-rejected` — the complete claim has not survived textual review.
- `wording-clarification-needed` — the proposition itself must be stated more precisely before evaluation.

## Required record boundaries

Each assumption must keep these fields separate:

1. `submitted_claim` — the proposition being tested, preserved without silently strengthening it.
2. `explicit_data` — statements Scripture explicitly gives.
3. `candidate_passages` — places to quarry; listing them does not establish identity.
4. `missing_links` — propositions required to prove the complete claim.
5. `chronology_effect` — what placement would change if the claim were established.
6. `resolution` — left empty until evidence review supports a decision.

## Governing rule

Assumption records may inform research priorities, but they may not populate the settled chronological spine as facts. Only source records and classified connections can do that.
